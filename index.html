About Me

Hi and thanks for visiting my Portfolio! I'm a games programming student currenty studying at Anglia Ruskin University and I love building gameplay mechanics and systems. Since I was young I've loved playing games, but through playing creative platforms like Minecraft I realised I also loved creating.

My first real experiences of development were using Minecraft's command system to build minigames for me and my friends, or creating mechanics that could be fun playing on a server. Once I had a good enough computer I shifted over to creating addons for Minecraft Bedrock Edition using their mod tools, and this got me hooked on developing my own content. I have been working with Unity and C# for around 4 years, and more recently Unreal Engine 5 and C++ for around 2 years, but I am always open to learning new skills and software plus I always find it fun to dive into new things.
