Details

Cannon Clash was a small project me and a group of two others made for Itch's GBJam14. The project's main inspiration was the Plants Vs Zombies tower defence series, with their lane based unit placement, with the twist of what if the enemy could also place plants rather than sending zombies against you. The goal is to place down enough offensive or defensive units to defeat your enemy before they can do the same to you, with a total of 5 unique units available.

Contributions

  • Universal input handler (works on any object given an input class)
  • Pause and setting menu UI and functionality
  • Screen tint switching
  • Save system
  • Unit placement and selection
  • Enemy AI behaviour
  • Menu backgrounds (excluding starting menu)
  • Health and gold systems
